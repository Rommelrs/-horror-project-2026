# AL-SARAB — Story Brief for Narrative

*Written 2026-11-14 from the project itself (Unity scenes, cutscene timelines, subtitle/localization tables, note pages), not from memory. Lines in quotes are the exact in-game English text. Anything marked **(inference)** is my reading of the material, not something the game states.*

---

## 1. Premise in one paragraph

Khalid comes back to **Al-Sarab**, the neighbourhood he grew up in, because of an anonymous message that mentioned **Adel** — his little brother. He crashes his car in a thick, unnatural fog; his phone is dead and his watch has stopped. A boy with a backpack he recognises runs past, and Khalid chases him into a town where time seems frozen, the streets are full of creatures, and notes, drawings and voices keep pulling him back toward something that happened with Adel years ago — something the notes insist he does not remember correctly ("*Don't run away this time.*").

### How the demo is built (play order)

| # | Scene | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Opening** | Khalid's monologue in the car about the message. |
| 2 | **Game** (open neighbourhood) | Crash in the fog → the boy → the grocery store door. |
| 3 | **Store** | The grocery store level (locked in, blackout, fuse, first Questioner, steel pipe). |
| 4 | **Game** again | Out the store's back → park, Alley 1 (coward meeting), Alley 2 (the "alley by the school"), between Alleys 2–3, Alley 3. |
| 5 | **End Demo** | Opening the barber-side door in Alley 3 ends the demo: "*Thanks for playing demo — Support AL-SARAB*". |

The **Barber** scene exists in the project but is **not** in the build yet — it is the next chapter.

## 2. Characters

**Khalid** — the player. Grew up in Al-Sarab, left, has "*wanted an excuse to come back here for years*". Calls Adel "*my brother*". Dry, tired humour under stress ("*Do I focus on the crash... or how thick this fog is?*"). Carries guilt he hasn't named; the Truth Note says he ran away once before.

**Adel** — Khalid's little brother (Khalid tells the coward "*He's my brother*"; the Truth Note says "*Something about the boy*"). A boy with a **blue backpack**. Draws "little adventures": a man with a gun "*saving his little brother from the monsters*". Loved **Al-Sarab Park** more than home; home felt "*quiet in the bad way*". Only ever seen at a distance or heard; in Alley 3 Khalid shouts "*Is that you, Adel??*".

**The coward** — a terrified local man met in Alley 1 (cutscene). Insists there are "*other versions of me out there… They all carry knives… they're ruining my reputation!*". Has seen the boy with the blue backpack heading to "*the alley by the school*". Begs Khalid to leave town; Khalid refuses. (The Runner enemies use the coward's face/model — see Enemies.)

**Samir** — grocery store worker/manager; wrote the Training Note (register code is on the side panel).

**Mr. Sultan** — owes the grocery an overdue payment, "*haven't seen him in two weeks*". The map has a **Sheikh Sultan Home**.

**The father** — never seen. Questioners whisper "*Where's your father?*" in the park; the bloody note's voice says "*Bring back the key you took from us*". **(inference)** A strong candidate for whatever "really" happened at home.

**Unnamed note writers** — a neighbour who saw a man flee a house at night; a trapped man warning others; a grocery shopper who built a shortcut over the broken road.

## 3. The world: Al-Sarab

**Map landmarks** (English map): Al-Mitar Street, Al-Sarab Street, Ahmed Street, Tal Street, Al-Kathlan Street; **Emerald Saloon** (the barber), **Sheikh Sultan Home**, **Asur Clinic**, **Maqr Cafeteria**, **Baidaa School**, **Al-Sarab Park**. The shop sign on the grocery reads **DUKAN / دكان**.

**Rules of the place (as shown):**
- A sand-coloured fog everywhere; time is stuck ("*Weird… why did my watch stop?*"; note: "*The clocks don't move anymore.*").
- Phones don't work.
- The town loops on people: the trapped man followed a boy's voice and found "*just another fog-covered house that looked like mine*".
- **Prayer beads** are the save points ("*Finding a prayer bead — Makes me feel comfortable*").
- **Stability** (sanity) is a stat: Stable → Unsteady → Shaken → Disturbed → Fractured. Pills restore it.
- Doors are often sealed with **mud bags** thrown by Bag Bearers — Khalid shoots or breaks them ("*This bag is held tight by the door*").
- Adel's drawings double as combat hints (the "Combat Hint" notes).

## 4. Story walkthrough (beat by beat)

### 4.1 Opening — in the car
Monologue, in order:
> "That message I got while I was sleeping in the car…." / "I couldn't sleep peacefully because of it." / "I still don't know who sent it." / "But it mentioned Adel." / "I've wanted an excuse to come back here for years." / "Guess I finally got one." / "Al-Sarab..." / "This place carries memories I can never forget." / "If whoever sent that message really knows something about Adel..." / "I need to find out what."

### 4.2 The crash (Game scene start)
> "Ah man... what the hell just happened?" / "Do I focus on the crash... or how thick this fog is?" / "Great. Even the phone isn't working…" / "Weird… why did my watch stop?"

A kid's voice: "*Heeeey watch where you're going!!*" — Khalid: "*What?? Who's there??*" / "*Haven't I seen that backpack before?*" / "*I have to chase him!*"

Near the start Khalid can find the **Truth Note** (the message that brought him here) and a note reading "*The kid is waiting for you at the grocery store*". If he wanders the wrong way: "*I have to find the kid first*".

### 4.3 The grocery store (Store scene)
The front door is sealed by a mud bag ("*I might be able to shoot it*"). Inside:
1. The lights flicker. Browsing gets "*Don't think it's time for shopping*". A shopping basket: "*Chicken breast and some soup — There's also a grocery list*".
2. Footsteps outside; a figure **locks Khalid in** ("*What the...? The door is locked! I better find a way out*"). Leaving is refused: "*I can't leave now... he could be there.*"
3. **Blackout**, silence, a crash from the storage room; "where is he" whispers, things falling.
4. The cash register needs power ("*Looks like the cash register requires power*"); the fuse box "*misses something*". Khalid searches cabinets for a fuse ("*Nothing useful.*" / "*Still no fuse...*"), finds it, restores partial power ("*The power is back on*").
5. The register code is on the side panel (Training Note). The register holds **the map of Al-Sarab** and **a key** ("*And also a key... not sure what for*"). The map has hand-drawn markers ("*What are these markers? Might be worth checking it out*").
6. Storage room: a **steel pipe** ("*A steel pipe... better than nothing.*"). Boxes burst open — **the first Questioner** ("*What kind of monster is this?*"). After killing it: "*It might be a good idea to leave the city but I don't think I can*".
7. Out through the back door, sealed by another bag ("*I might be able to break it.*").

### 4.4 Back outside — after the store
A short cutscene: "*Oh my god....*" / "*What is that in front of me?!*" — the town has turned hostile.

### 4.5 Al-Sarab Park
The emotional centre of Adel's thread:
- A board with a child's drawing: "*This drawing style is very familiar — Maybe a child used it to find his way to home*".
- Questioner voices: "*" Where's your father? "*" — Khalid: "*I don't like this*".
- "*Someone drilled a hole here*" / "*...*"
- Crayons ("*Someone used to love them*"), a plastic tray.
- Adel's diary in two halves (**Message 1st half / 2nd half**), the first **Combat Hint** drawing, and the **Disturbing Note**.
- A chalk map piece (second map page).

### 4.6 Alley 1 — the rope gate, the knife, the coward
- Ropes block the way; a **lighter** burns them.
- A **knife handle** with a note: "*Come get the other half. I'll be waiting for you between Alley 2 and 3.*"
- Carrying it triggers **the coward meeting** (cutscene), at the crossroads past the burned rope gate. He begs Khalid not to shoot ("*Wait, wait! Don't shoot!*" — or with only the pipe, "*It's not me, I swear it's not me!*").
  - Khalid: "*You... You locked me in that store. You tried to kill me!*"
  - Coward: "*It wasn't me! I swear on my life, it wasn't! There are other versions of me out there. I don't know why! They all carry knives... they're ruining my reputation!*"
  - He offers to lead Khalid back to the main road; Khalid: "*I'm not leaving. Have you seen a kid with a blue backpack?*" — "*Yeah. I think so. Through the fog, I couldn't see him well. Why? Who is he to you?*" — "*He's my brother. Where did he go?*"
  - **Version A** (Khalid hasn't been to Alley 2): "*The alley by the school. But listen to me, you can't go after him! I barely got out of there in one piece.*" Khalid: "*Thanks.*" — "*Wait! He went in there hours ago! He could already be—*"
  - **Version B** (Khalid already survived Alley 2): Khalid: "*I've already been in there.*" — "*...You went in? And you came back out alive?? This is impossible... Have you seen them??*" — "*Yeah...*" — "*I'm sorry to tell you this, man, but your brother could've... the monsters... they... We should definitely escape this town, man—*" Khalid: "*Thanks.*"
  - Ending (both): "*HEY! I'M TRYING TO SAVE YOUR LIFE HERE!*" … "*You're insane...*"

### 4.7 Alley 2 — "the alley by the school"
- The **pistol** is found at the alley's mouth ("*It's a gun, I hope I won't have to use it*").
- The entrance locks behind him ("*What the...?!! The door is locked!!*").
- **The horde**: a line of Questioners screams in chorus and attacks in waves. A **gate key** ("*Opens the main door for Alley 2*") is the way out.
- Notes here: **Family Note** (the shortcut over the broken road) and **Man warning others**.

### 4.8 Between Alleys 2 and 3
Broken bridges; **wooden planks** fix them ("*This bridge looks broken*" / "*Now I can pass here*"). Runners hunt this stretch. (The knife note's meeting point is here.)

### 4.9 Alley 3
- A cloth blocks the way; the knife cuts it ("*What the...? This is really getting creepy*").
- **Khalid sees a figure — "*Is that you, Adel??*"** — and is knocked over a table by a Runner; on the ground: "*Must kill them first!*"
- **The hole in the wall**: "*What is this hole? For some reason, this wall makes me uncomfortable...*" / "*I can't stand near this hole anymore!!*" Duct tape seals it.
- **A bloody note** held by something: "*" Want to silence the hole? " " We have what you need " " Bring back the key you took from us " " And this time... answer our question "*" — Khalid: "*Seriously what's going on here?!*"
- A blood-written code on a wall (combination lock); a **Bag Bearer** encounter; the **Barber Key** behind a circuit box.
- Notes: **Man hearing someone**, the last **Combat Hint**, and (at the barber front) the **Barber Notice**.

### 4.10 The barber door — end of demo
The barber-side door is "*it's locked*" until Khalid has the Barber Key; opening it ends the demo.

**Training Note** — *Training note: If a customer looks confused, step in and help immediately. Don't leave them hanging, it makes the store look bad. And for the love of God, the register code is written on the side panel. Stop forgetting it. — Samir*

**Storage Room Reminder** — *Reminder: If Mr. Sultan comes by, ask about his overdue payment. I haven't seen him in two weeks. Also whoever's stocking the dairy section keeps disappearing. I asked for help twice and the boy just stood there staring. Weird day.*

**Truth Note** — *Khalid, You already know why Al-Sarab still bothers you. You keep telling yourself that you remember what happened. You don't. There's something you missed. Something about the boy. Go back to Al-Sarab. Start from the places you both used to know. The grocery. The streets near your house. Follow what you remember, even if it doesn't make sense at first. And when you start seeing him... Don't run away this time. You've done that enough.*

**"Man is lost in city"** — *The kid is waiting for you at the grocery store*

**Message 1st half** — *I like Al Sarab Park more than anywhere else. After school, me and the boys go straight there. We stop by the cafeteria first, buy some good poratta's with chips and hot sauce wow it's really good, then sit on the benches like we own the place. We laugh about stupid stuff, talk about teachers, talk about nothing, and the sun starts going down and everything feels… normal. The park makes my chest feel lighter.*

**Message 2nd half** — *At home it's different. Home is quiet in the bad way. the air is always heavy and I'm always waiting for something to happen even when nothing is happening. I hate that feeling. In the park, I don't think about any of that. I just sit there, hear the wind in the trees, and for a little while… it feels like I'm not trapped in my own life. Sometimes I really want to sleep in the park but don't want people to pity me.*

**Disturbing Note** — *THE CURIOSITY WILL KILL YOU AGAIN :)*

**Family Note** — *I'm done taking the long way to the grocery. Every time I go through those alleys, someone wants to stop and talk. Maintenance still hasn't fixed the broken road, so I made my own way across. Much quicker. Much quieter.*

**Man warning others** — *To whoever finds this. I've been here for… I don't know. The clocks don't move anymore. The air feels heavy. I keep hearing a boy calling for help. I followed him once, there was no boy. Just another fog-covered house that looked like mine. Please, if you came looking for someone, turn back. There's nothing left of them. Or you*

**Man hearing someone** — *I woke up because of a door slamming so hard it shook the glass. I looked out. A man burst out of the house and ran like he was being chased. Not drunk. Not lost. Afraid. He kept looking back over his shoulder like he'd done something terrible. He dropped his keys, cursed under his breath, hands shaking. Got in the car, started it, and peeled out without even turning the lights on at first. He drove off fast and that kinda scared me*

**Combat Hint 1** — *This style… it's familiar. Adel always drew like this. Messy lines, strange monsters, and that same old sun in the corner. I remember asking him once why he kept drawing everything this way. He just smiled and said, "Because it looks better like this." It still looks childish… but somehow, I understand what he was trying to show me.*

**Combat Hint 2** — *Why is a drawing like this here? I don't know if Adel drew it, or one of his friends did… but it has the same style. He used to draw these little adventures all the time. I remember asking him why all the monsters looked the same? Later, he started changing their faces and bodies a little. I guess he was listening. I started liking his drawings after that. He really was talented.*

**Combat Hint 3** — *Yeah… this one is definitely Adel's. Those faces gave it away. Same eyes. Same teeth. Same angry little creatures marching in a line. I used to point that out to him all the time. "Adel, why do they all look the same?" He never got mad. He'd just stare at the page, nod like I gave him some serious advice, then add a scar… or a different eye… or make one taller than the rest. One day I asked him, "Who's the guy with the gun?" He got excited and said, "He's saving his little brother from the monsters." I laughed, it sounded so stupid back then. Now I wish I asked him more about it.*

**Combat Hint 4** — *One of Adel's Drawings. This one felt different. I remember asking Adel why the man with the gun looked so angry this time. He told me, "Because his little brother died. The monsters ate him… so now he's surrounded by rage." I remember just staring at him after that. I always thought children gave their stories happy endings. Heroes win, monsters lose, everyone goes home. But Adel's little adventures didn't always end like that. Sometimes he said things that made him feel older than he was. I never minded dark stories… but hearing them come from a kid always felt strange.*

**Barber Notice** — *Kids have been entering the barber to get free candies instead of shaving so instead of leaving the door open, I decided to lock it, any coworkers there you should find the key in the circuit box, be sure to lock it afterwards and leave it at your car at least*

**Child Drawing** — an image only (no text page); Khalid: "*This drawing style is very familiar*".

## 7. Threads and mysteries the demo sets up

**Stated facts:**
- Someone sent Khalid a message about Adel; the Truth Note (addressed to Khalid by name) says he misremembers what happened, that it's "about the boy", and that he ran away before.
- Adel's drawings show a man with a gun saving his little brother — and, in the last one, raging because "his little brother died. The monsters ate him."
- Adel felt safe in the park and trapped at home.
- A neighbour saw a man flee a house at night, terrified, "like he'd done something terrible".
- The town wants something back: "*Bring back the key you took from us… And this time… answer our question.*"
- Khalid already picks up keys everywhere (the register key "not sure what for", gate key, barber key).
- The Runners wear the coward's face; the coward swears they aren't him.

**(inference) Open questions a writer will want to decide:**
- Who is "the guy with the gun" in Adel's drawings — Khalid? And who is "the little brother" — is Adel the older one in the drawings' logic, which would flip what Khalid believes?
- Who ran from the house in "Man hearing someone" — Khalid years ago, or the father?
- Which key was taken "from us", and what is the Questioners' question — "*Where's your father?*"
- Is the town purgatory? ("*Maybe I died in that crash*" exists as a written but disabled line.)
- Why does the coward have doubles, and why do they all carry knives?

## 8. Inconsistencies and loose ends (for the writer to fix or use)

1. **Disturbing Note** — the page says "*THE CURIOSITY WILL KILL YOU AGAIN :)*", but its inventory description quotes "*The truth should not stay buried.*"
2. **Family Note** — description "Someone really loved his family but it was too late..." doesn't match its text (a shortcut over the broken road). Another key, "Missing Family note — Someone really misses his family", exists with no placed note.
3. **"Man is lost in city"** — the item name doesn't match its one line ("*The kid is waiting for you at the grocery store*").
4. **Who locked Khalid in the store?** Earlier design notes describe "a distorted Khalid", but the coward cutscene makes it one of the coward's doubles. Pick one.
5. **"Adel" vs "the boy"** — Khalid says "He's my brother" to the coward, while the drawings' "little brother" framing could point either way. Decide who is older.
6. **Two drafts of the coward conversation** exist in the subtitle table (an older "Don't read those out loud. Something always answers." / "...Then something let you." version). Only the current Open/A/B/End timelines play.
7. **Map spelling** — "Maqr Cafteria" (Cafeteria?).
8. **Typos in lines** — "it stop halfway through", "This is map for sarab", "poratta's".
